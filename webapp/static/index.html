How Lekhak reads a page

      
Three steps, all on this machine. Nothing is uploaded to a third-party service.

      

        

          
Find the text
A text detector locates every line and word on the page, then orders them the way you would read them.

        

          
Read in three languages
Separate English, Tamil and Hindi recognisers read the text. The Tamil and Hindi ones are adapted to handwriting.

        

          
Pick the right script
A small learned classifier weighs all three readings and chooses the script — for the whole image or line by line.
